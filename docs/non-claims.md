# Non-Claims

Chip Heat Lab is a simplified early-design thermal intuition demo. It is not:

- signoff
- JEDEC evidence
- foundry validation
- CFD
- package co-design
- reliability analysis
- HotSpot parity
- 3D-ICE parity
- commercial parity
- an accurate chip thermal simulator

Do not use Chip Heat Lab output as approval, verification, or manufacturing
evidence. The demo exists to make a small concept visible and explainable during
a hackathon walkthrough.
