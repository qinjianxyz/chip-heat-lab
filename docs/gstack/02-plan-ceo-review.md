# 02 CEO Review

## Positioning

Use the tagline "engineering simulation for chip design" but immediately qualify
the artifact as a simplified early-design thermal intuition demo.

## Demo Promise

The viewer should leave understanding that simulation-style feedback can be
interactive and explainable without claiming production authority.

## Risk

The main product risk is overclaiming. The README, app panel, site, KB, and lint
script all route the demo back to assumptions and non-claims.
